# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the DoodleNote community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while remaining clear about technical and community impact.
- Give and accept specific, actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy. Never post another person's meeting content, credentials, or personal information.
- Focus discussion on what is best for users and the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, sexualized language, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or personal and political attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the Onyx Dev Labs organization owners through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
