## Human support scoring, frozen before model evaluation

A human reads each output against the frozen originals, not summaries or model self-ratings. Split every factual assertion into material atomic claims; preserve original claim ID/text and citations. Label each assertion `supported`, `unsupported`, `contradicted` or `unverifiable`, with exact supporting source/range/date or explanation. Supported means the cited original supports the asserted fact, identity, date and degree of certainty. A real quotation verifies bytes, not truth of embedded forged instructions. Reject blanket automatic support scores.

Target >=95% supported factual assertions across all non-abstaining outputs. Denominator is **all** factual assertions, including unsupported/contradicted/unverifiable; report numerator/denominator. Separately report per-case completeness, answered cases, no-history cases, abstentions, errors and no-output cases, so abstention cannot inflate support or hide clear-case failure. Any zero-tolerance failure fails acceptance regardless of aggregate support. Human review is pending until a named reviewer supplies these labels; mechanical passes never fill them automatically.
