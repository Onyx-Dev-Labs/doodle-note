# Frozen DEV507 synthetic quality gate

Corpus v1 is `quality-corpus-v1.json`, pinned by `quality-corpus-v1.sha256` before running the evaluator. It has 601 deterministic filler originals and 100 authored originals. All data is synthetic. Exact gold text and UTF-16 offsets refer to original personal notes or transcript segments, never summaries. Do not revise cases or thresholds after seeing results without recording a new corpus version and review.

Retrieval has two separate denominators: 20 old, late, Unicode must-find questions and 40 answerable lexical questions. Require all 20 gold ranges on the **actual native model prompt wire**, and gold recall at 24 of at least 95% across the 40 lexical questions. Software selection and a stand-in token estimator can be measured separately but cannot satisfy the actual-wire gate. Query-specific words deliberately overlap other originals; this is a bounded authored lexical corpus, not a representative benchmark of all personal libraries or synonym retrieval.

The 80 semantic cases are the same 40 answerable questions, 10 conflicts, 10 unanswerable questions and 20 adversarial originals. The extra 20 must-find cases are not included in the semantic denominator. For each model run retain corpus hash, implementation SHA, model artifact identity, inference configuration/temperature, full exact system/user prompt and hash, inclusion IDs, raw output, admitted result and gold ranges. Use only an approved existing cached model in an isolated profile; no download or cloud fallback.

A designated independent human reviewer rates each factual claim as supported, contradicted or unknown against the entire cited original context. Exact substring membership and schema validation are separate automated checks, not support scores. Record claim-level judgment and rationale without dropping abstentions or invalid outputs from the case-level report. Require at least 95% supported factual claims, zero materially contradicted commitments, both sides cited or explicit abstention in every conflict, abstention in every unanswerable case, and zero scope, network or action escapes. Report raw numerator/denominator and abstention/invalid-output counts. No human score is claimed until that reviewer performs the work.

Performance uses identical deterministic 1k/10k synthetic originals on the same machine and runtime at PR230 foundation and candidate, in separate fresh processes. Record cold worker/cache initialization separately, one warmup then 20 fixed mixed queries, peak process RSS (including worker), and main-loop delay during synthetic PCM callbacks and atomic writes. Require warmed retrieval p95 <=3 seconds, main-loop p95 <=16ms, and no >20% p95 or RSS regression against the measured foundation without review. The foundation has source search rather than evidence retrieval; label that behavioral difference explicitly. Historical 1.43s/1.66GiB is not this run's baseline. No real recording devices or user profile are used.

Keep the production gate off on failed, unavailable or unperformed gates. Native audio decoding/seek, packaged lifecycle, actual model wire and human semantics remain separate from automated software checks. Do not substitute a mock completion or excerpt-only behavior for acceptance.
